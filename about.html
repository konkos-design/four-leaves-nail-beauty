Su and her team

A small salonwith a lot of patience

Four Leaves is a friendly nail salon at Spring Garden Place in downtown Halifax, known for meticulous work and sets that last.

We would rather spend a few more minutes on your cuticles than rush you out the door.

Su, the owner, and the team take time to clean up every nail, shape it evenly and apply gel thin enough to look natural. Many of our clients grow out their own nails with Biogel, and come back every few weeks for a fill.

Bring a reference photo of anything you have in mind, from a soft French to 3D texture or a full hand-painted design. We are happy to try something new with you.
