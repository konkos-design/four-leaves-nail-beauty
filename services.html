Before you book

Salon policy

A few house rules that keep every appointment on time and every set looking its best.

  1. Shellac and gel only. We do not offer regular polish or acrylic services.
  2. Age. For health and safety reasons we may not accept clients under 12. Clients aged 12-18 come with a parent or legal guardian.
  3. Timing. We may refuse or cancel service for clients who arrive more than 15 minutes late or repeatedly miss appointments without notice.
  4. Nail health. We cannot safely service fungal or ingrown nails.
  5. Other salons. We do not repair or remove enhancements applied by other salons. Our removal uses both an electric file and soak-off, soak-off alone is not available.
  6. Final sale. All services and gift certificates are final sale and non-refundable.
